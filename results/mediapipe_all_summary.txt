Basis and methodology reminders
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Sections 4 and 5 use the full-window basis; section 6 uses the last-20%-of-each-segment basis. Absolute values must not be subtracted.
  2. Arms from different chunks (different runs) must not be subtracted (cross-run dispersion ≈0.025).
  3. |diff| < 0.02 counts as “indistinguishable”; **do not write “identical”** — that requires TOST.
  4. Public material always uses S01–S10; real names or nicknames must never appear.
